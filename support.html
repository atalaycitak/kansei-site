Support

For bug reports, suggestions or questions, send an email to atalaycitakdev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I drive?

The car accelerates by itself. Touch anywhere and drag sideways to steer: drag right and the car swings left, drag left and it swings right; the further you drag, the deeper the slide. Push your thumb up for nitro, and lift it to straighten the car. Drifting fills the nitro bar.

How does scoring work?

Drift points build up in a chain while you slide. Every left-right switch raises the multiplier, up to ×6. Drive straight for a moment and the chain is banked. Crash before that and the unbanked chain is lost. The highest score after 60 seconds wins the duel.

I bought something but don't have it

Open Settings › RESTORE PURCHASES in the game. NO ADS comes back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Coin packs are added once, when the purchase completes; if the game closed during a purchase, the coins are added the next time you open it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved only on your device, so it does not move to a new device and is deleted with the app. NO ADS comes back with RESTORE PURCHASES.

There is no sound

Check that SOUND is ON in the game's Settings and that your media volume is up.

Are my rivals real players?

No. Every rival is a computer-controlled driver matched to your rating and car; the game plays fully offline.

I closed the app during a duel and lost rating

A rated duel that is left before the finish counts as a loss, whether through ‹ MENU or by closing the app.
