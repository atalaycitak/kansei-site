Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Kansei is a drift racing game for iPhone. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. In short: your progress stays on your device, we run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves, and the game shows ads provided by third-party advertising partners, who collect some device data to show and measure those ads.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — the driver name and avatar you pick, coins, cars, upgrades, rating, match statistics and settings — is saved in a file on your device only. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Rivals in the game are computer-controlled drivers. Your driver name is not shown to other people.

Advertising

Kansei is free to play and shows ads, for example a full-screen ad between races and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. These ads are served by third-party advertising partners. When an ad is requested or shown, the partner's software in the game may collect and process:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system and version, language, app version);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or region);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, tapped or watched) and diagnostic data such as crashes of the ad software;
  • the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow it when iOS asks for permission to track. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using the IDFA.

Partners use this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed tracking, show ads based on your interests. You can change the tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and the game's Settings → AD CHOICES lets you change it later. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

The NO ADS purchase removes the ads between races. Optional reward ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game checks on your device that a purchase is genuine and keeps its transaction number in your save so it is credited once. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or server of our own; we do not receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Children

Kansei is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, requests about ad data are best sent to the advertising partners above; we are happy to help — write to the address below.

Changes
